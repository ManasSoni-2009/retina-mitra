# Safety, Ethics, and Clinical Disclaimers

## Mandatory Principles
1. **Decision Support Only:** DrishtiSetu is an AI screening assistant and clinical decision support tool. It is NOT an autonomous medical diagnostic device.
2. **Standardized Terminology:**
   - **Allowed Terms:** "AI screening result", "decision support recommendation", "refer for ophthalmologist evaluation", "image ungradable", "calibrated confidence score".
   - **Prohibited Terms:** "definitely has DR", "doctor replacement", "100% accurate", "confirmed diagnosis".
3. **Patient Data Privacy:** No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is stored or displayed in demonstration data. All patient identifiers are synthetic or anonymized (`PAT-2026-XXXX`).
4. **Mandatory Human Escalation:** High entropy, low confidence, or ungradable images force an automated escalation prompt requiring human specialist review.
